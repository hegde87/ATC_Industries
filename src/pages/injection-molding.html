Injection Molding

Precision plastic components manufactured in engineering polymers for appliance and industrial OEMs.

About this process

Injection molding produces repeatable, dimensionally consistent parts at volume. We mold components such as insert-molded tubes, gaskets, housings, filter guards and precision fasteners to customer drawings, selecting the polymer to suit the application.

Annual capacity
500 MT / annum
Press range
50T – 250T
Materials
PA6, PA6,6, PC, PP, PE, SAN, ABS, HIPS, PU, TPE
Typical parts
Housings, insert-molded tubes, gaskets, filter guards, fittings and fasteners
