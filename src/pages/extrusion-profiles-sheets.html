Extrusion of Profiles and Sheets

Plastic profiles, sheets, tubes and corrugated hoses produced for application-specific requirements.

{{ figure "approved-orange-extrusion-hero.jpg" alt="ATC plastic extrusion line with orange extruder" sizes="(min-width: 64rem) 60vw, 100vw" group=extrusion fit=wide }}

About this process

Continuous extrusion lines produce profiles, tubes and sheets to a consistent cross-section. With corrugation equipment we also make flexible hoses — including washing-machine exhaust and outlet hoses and split AC drain pipes.

Annual capacity
300 MT / annum
Equipment
Extrusion lines with corrugation
Typical parts
Profiles, tubes, multi-lumen and specialty tubes, door gaskets, corrugated and flexible hoses
