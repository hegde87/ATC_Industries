Manufacturing with purpose since 1996.

Founded under the legacy of the late Mr. A. T. Shah, ATC Industries is a partnership concern and part of The Shah Group of Companies.

Our multi-location approach and range of plastic processing capabilities support OEM requirements in home appliances, telecom equipment and automotive applications — from injection-molded parts and extruded profiles to thermoformed radomes and welded assemblies.
