About this process

Blow molding inflates a heated plastic parison inside a mold to form hollow, seamless parts. It is well suited to bellows, ducts, tanks and containers where a closed or flexible form is needed.

Annual capacity
40 MT / annum
Typical parts
Bellows for top-loading washing machines and other hollow components
