CONTRACT MANUFACTURING
+ ASSEMBLY

From custom component manufacturing to assembly and kitting, ATC Industries works with customers to explore manufacturing solutions tailored to their specifications, material requirements and applications.

Contract Manufacturing

ATC Industries offers contract manufacturing solutions beyond plastic components. We work with customers to explore customized parts and products based on their drawings, technical specifications, material requirements and intended applications.

  • Custom component manufacturing
  • Manufacturing to customer drawings and specifications
  • Plastic and non-plastic component requirements
  • Material and process evaluation
  • Prototype and production feasibility discussions

Assembly

We support customers with component preparation, joining and assembly requirements, helping bring individual manufactured parts together into application-ready solutions.

  • Component preparation and cutting
  • High-frequency plastic welding
  • Fabricated and assembled components
  • Customer-specific assembly requirements
  • Assembly feasibility and process discussions

Kitting

ATC Industries can support customer-specific kitting requirements by organizing multiple components into defined sets or kits based on customer specifications, helping simplify material handling and downstream assembly operations.

  • Customer-specific component kits
  • Component grouping and segregation
  • Kit preparation as per customer requirements
  • Packaging and identification requirements
  • Order-wise or assembly-wise kit preparation
